Version 2 (29th December)

- Added:
  - `wild_pokemon_encounter` function to manage Pokémon encounters and catching mechanics.
  - `battle` function to simulate battles between players Pokémon and wild Pokémon.
  - `view_pokemon` function in the Player class to allow viewing of caught Pokémon.
  - Method to allow players to choose from three starter Pokémon (Pikachu, Charmander,       Bulbasaur) at the start of the game.
  - Option in the main menu for players to choose between catching or fighting a wild Pokémon during encounters.
  - Expanded list of available wild Pokémon to include ten different Pokémon.


- Removed:
  - Simplified mechanics for randomly catching wild Pokémon, no longer purely chance-based.
  
- Changed:
  - Main menu to include "View Pokémon" option.
  - Player starts the game by choosing one of three starter Pokémon.
  - Wild Pokémon encounters now provide an option to either catch or battle the wild Pokémon.
